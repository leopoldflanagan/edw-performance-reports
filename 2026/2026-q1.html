Sprint metrics — Q1 2026
The seven sprints that closed inside the quarter, rebuilt from Jira.
What the Q1 sprints show

Every sprint delivered between 100% and 156% of what it committed to on day 1.

Scope grew between +0% and +56% mid-sprint — modest next to the +76% to +279% the same board shows from April on. The pattern that later becomes a problem is already here in a milder form: the team commits low and fills the sprint as it runs, so completion against commitment reads above 100% while the commitment itself means little. Average delivered across the seven sprints is 52.6 points, though one sprint (R9.00-S5, 111 points) carries most of January on its own.

Findings & retro
What to take from the quarter, and from the correction.
Quarter takeaway

Q1 was a stable quarter. The reason nobody could tell is that three different numbers were circulating for the same metric.

40 deliveries a month with an 8-item spread, unplanned work comfortably under target and cycle time close to it. As a baseline it holds — with the caveat that it describes a 4-developer team, which EDW no longer is.

How these figures were reconciled

Method

The Q1 report published in Confluence carried two different throughput figures: 41 items/month in its executive summary, and 100 closed / 33.3 per month (10 → 45 → 45) in its detail section. Neither could be reproduced from Jira under any filter definition — January returns between 32 and 51 in every variant tested. The 100 matches the Cycle Time population instead: 102 of the 120 items closed in Q1 ever entered In Development, and the Cycle Time section is built on 99 issues. The same denominator explains the unplanned figure: the published 4.9% is 5 items over 102, where over 120 it is 4.17%. Both sit under target, so that status is unchanged. The Confluence page now carries the corrected figures.

Reconciled: September 2026

Retire one of the two throughput filters

Action

The dashboard gadget reads EDW – Throughput – Previous Month (Resolved) (id 14345), which has no resolution clause and counts Won't Do items — Q1 gives 124. EDW – Throughput – Last Month (id 14344) restricts to issuetype in (Bug, Story) — Q1 gives 122. The clean definition gives 120. While all three exist, the same metric reports a different number depending on where it is read. Database Services has the identical duplicated pair (ids 15837 and 16212).

For action by: EDW + DS

Decide what Cycle Time counts

Discuss

The published 6.29d / 7.83d and the recalculated 7.24d / 18.49d come from the same data read two ways. The monthly reports use the second method, so until this is settled, Q1 is not strictly comparable to the months that follow it.

For discussion with: EDW

Commit to something closer to what gets delivered

Discuss

Five of the seven Q1 sprints closed above 110% of their day-1 commitment. Reading that as over-performance is tempting and wrong: it means the sprint is planned after it starts. It is mild here and severe by August, so Q1 is where the habit is visible without the noise.

For discussion with: EDW

Fix the sprint naming

Follow-up

EDW-Sprint 8-27 should be 8-26. Cheap to fix, and it is the same slip DS has with 16-27 — worth a shared naming convention rather than two separate corrections.

For follow-up by: EDW + DS
