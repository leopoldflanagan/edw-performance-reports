Observation of the month

Most of May's Unplanned work shares a common source: reporting requests.

Of the 7 items labeled Unplanned, 5 (71%) were Finance/Accounting reporting requests. This is an observation from the data — the team is best placed to interpret what drove it and whether any of it could be anticipated. The points below are starting points for that conversation, not conclusions.

Possible directions to explore
Ideas the team might consider in the retro — to validate, adapt, or set aside based on the real context.

Look at the Finance reporting requests together

Discuss

Since reporting requests made up most of the Unplanned work this month, it may be worth reviewing them as a group. The team would know whether some of these tend to recur or could be anticipated — and if so, whether they could be brought into planning ahead of time.

For discussion with: EDW + Finance

Consider whether any recurring work can be simplified

Discuss

If any of these requests turn out to repeat regularly, the team might explore whether they could be made easier to run or handed off as self-service — reducing how often they come back as new work. Only the team knows which (if any) fit this.

For discussion with: EDW

Explore an intake agreement with Finance

Discuss

If predictability is a shared concern, the team and Finance could discuss a lightweight way for reporting requests to arrive with some lead time, leaving room to fit them into a sprint. Whether this is needed is for both sides to decide.

For discussion with: EDW + Finance

Revisit capacity for reactive work

Discuss

For work that genuinely can't be planned ahead, the team might consider whether reserving a small amount of sprint capacity helps absorb it without affecting planned commitments. The right amount — if any — is something to feel out over a few sprints.

For discussion with: EDW

Keep labeling consistent & tidy up the data

Follow-up

Labeling consistency affects how comparable the numbers are month to month, so keeping the Unplanned label applied consistently helps the trend stay meaningful. Two open technical follow-ups: complete the donut's Rich Filter, and confirm the monthly saved filters use resolved rather than created.

For follow-up by: EDW
For the retrospective

Questions for the team

  • Looking at the 7 Unplanned items, how would we characterize them — were any of them things we could have seen coming?
  • Are there reporting requests that tend to repeat, and would it help to anticipate them in planning?
  • Would any recurring work benefit from being automated or simplified?
  • Does it feel like reactive work is affecting our planned commitments, and if so, how do we want to handle it?
  • With 2 new developers ramping up, when does it make sense to revisit the throughput baseline?
